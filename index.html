About Grabbo

Grabbo SNU isn't just a store; it's your ultimate campus lifeline. Located right at the heart of the Shopping Arcade in Shiv Nadar University, we exist to keep the campus running.

We know student life. We know the 2 AM assignment crunches that demand instant energy, the post-exam celebrations that call for a cold drink, and the sudden cravings for comfort food when you miss home. That's why we've curated a premium, hand-picked selection of your favorite snacks, exotic beverages, imported goodies, and daily essentials.

From steaming hot Maggi on a rainy day to the perfect iced coffee before a morning lecture—we constantly listen to the SNU community. At Grabbo, we don't just stock shelves; we deliver exactly what you need to keep your vibe alive, right when you need it most.
